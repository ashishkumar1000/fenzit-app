Employees can create jobs: pick the skill, customer and time slot; the job waits for owner approval.
Owners approve or reject employee jobs in the new Pending segment on Jobs, and can switch the feature off in job settings.
Rejected jobs show the owner's reason on the job.
Home shows admins a summary of employee jobs, linked to waiting approvals.
Times read as uppercase AM/PM app-wide, and "Add employee" and "Add customer" labels show in full on small phones.
