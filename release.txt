Fenzit app release 1.4.4 — employees create jobs, owners approve (2026-11-14)

Employees create jobs
- A new "New job" flow on the Jobs tab: pick the skill, the customer and
  the time slot, add a new customer on the way if needed, and the job is
  created as a request.
- The job goes to the owner for approval and the employee sees
  "Job created — waiting for approval". Owners can turn this ability on
  or off per employee in job settings.
- A job that is still waiting or was rejected stays quiet: no workflow
  bar on its detail screen, and the owner's reason is readable there.

The Pending queue (owners)
- Jobs has a new Pending segment listing employee-created jobs waiting
  for approval, with a count on the row so nothing hides.
- Approve from the job, or reject with a reason. The employee sees the
  reason on the job; deciding the same job twice is ignored and the
  list corrects itself.
- Every edit the owner makes to a pending job is recorded field by
  field, with the time of each change.

My jobs (employees)
- Profile opens into a "My jobs" record: a 30-day or 7-day window, the
  outcome counts, the approval rate, and jobs split into waiting and
  done, with the reasons for rejections readable right there. Pull to
  refresh.

Home
- Admins see a summary strip of employee-created jobs: 30-day counts
  and a row linking straight to the waiting approvals.
- The screen reads cleaner: section headings moved up a size rank, all
  times use uppercase AM/PM app-wide instead of the phone deciding, and
  the punch tiles, worked chip and job times keep fixed-width digits so
  the seconds no longer wiggle.
- The "Add employee" and "Add customer" tile labels no longer clip to
  their first word on some Android phones — they wrap to two lines.
