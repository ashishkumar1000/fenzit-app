Fenzit app release 1.4.0 — the punch day, redesigned (2026-11-11)

The punch button
- One blue circle for every posture — no more red failure face, no lock.
  State lives in the pill (READY / ON SHIFT), the sub-label and the cards.
  The dashed ring spins slowly while a punch is invited (respects
  reduce-motion) and freezes otherwise.
- The client geofence LOCK is gone. The button is always tappable once the
  day's facts are known; the press does its own strict location capture
  and the server decides. A blind press can never lock anyone out.

The punch card (owner mockups, 2026-11-10)
- Two edge-aligned tiles: CHECK IN and CHECK OUT, each reading big time →
  verdict. Green "On time", amber "Late by N min" / "Early by N min", red
  "Early by …" when the leave crossed the shift midpoint (half the shift
  or less worked), and a grey Pending pill before a punch lands.
- One WORKED chip under the divider, toned by the day: a full shift keeps
  the green ✓ celebration ("Full shift completed • Sched 9:30 AM - 6:30
  PM"); a short day drops the ✓, turns amber and captions the gap ("Short
  by 6h 31m"); a day with no schedule stays neutral ("Shift duration").
- Done state: no button, no status card — the tiles are the receipt.

Location problems now explain themselves
- Location services off: a RED blocker card (lost-pin motif) plus a tap-time
  popup in the app's own design system ("Location is off" — Turn on
  location / Not now). "Turn on location" deep-links to the system's
  Location screen.
- Permission denied: the plain system rationale popup is retired. Tapping
  opens OUR dialog first ("Location access is off" — Allow location / Not
  now); only its confirm fires the OS permission sheet.
- Distance failures keep their guidance card ("Too far from … Go closer,
  then check in.") — advice, never a gate.

Today tab
- The empty-day schedule card is COMPACT when attendance is enabled (the
  punch zone owns the attention) and keeps the big dashed-well card when
  attendance is disabled. The compact medallion carries a hand-drawn
  clipboard illustration.
- The green at-office card uses the owner's own words: "You're at the
  office — You're at <office>, just <N> meters away. Your location is
  verified."

Under the hood
- The fence prescreen is now pull-based: one snapshot capture on tab focus
  and on foreground return; no background watcher, ever. The press always
  captures fresh at the moment of truth.
- Expected capture failures (permission, services off) no longer paint dev
  builds red in the console.
